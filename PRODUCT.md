# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Astro (static output, no client framework). Chosen because the site is data-driven (one project list feeds the homepage, the work index, filters and case studies), ships as plain HTML/CSS with small vanilla scripts, and deploys to any static host such as GitHub Pages.

## Users

- Primary: people deciding whether to hire or commission the owner — recruiters, hiring managers, studio leads and prospective clients. They review many portfolios in a session, often for under a minute, and need to judge range, recency and fit for one discipline quickly.
- Inferred (not yet confirmed): a share of visits arrive on phones from a link in an application, message or social profile.

## Product Purpose

A personal design portfolio for one designer whose body of work will grow to many projects (the owner expects 20–40). Success: a visitor can see the whole range of work at a glance, narrow it to the discipline they care about, open any project in one step, and get back to where they were without losing their place.

## Positioning

The owner is Natalia Petrovska. Her statement: art direction, full-scale art production, and an AI gen pipeline on demand. She helps with four things, described in her own words (lightly corrected for grammar) in the About section: art direction, AI production, UI/UX design and mentoring. Her work is for games studios. Still undecided and still placeholder: the home headline and introduction, location, email and social links.

## Operating Context

- Visitors move between the index and individual projects repeatedly; returning to the same filter and scroll position matters.
- Projects come in two depths (owner's decision): 6–8 get full case-study pages; the rest open as a quick lightbox from the index.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Work index with discipline filters and counts (owner's decision: filterable grid).
- Case studies show position in the set, previous/next, and related projects.
- All content lives in one data file so projects can be added without touching layout.
- Undecided: real project list, disciplines, imagery, contact details, CV.

## Brand Commitments

- Owner's words: likes the style of designbymariam.com and chose "same calm base, own twist" — keep its calm grey/white base, generous spacing and restraint, but with the owner's own type and one signature detail so it does not read as a template. The reference site sets the craft bar and calm register; it is not to be copied.
- Owner's words: "I want the one from this website to match exactly" — typography must match designbymariam.com: the Apple system stack (SF Pro on Apple devices) with Inter as the fallback elsewhere, weights 400/500/600. SF Pro cannot be self-hosted under its licence, so Inter is the bundled fallback.

## Evidence on Hand

One real project: Next Games (puzzle cards on the Home screen of a puzzle games app, 2023). Its case study uses only what the owner's design file contains: the feature overview, UX drivers, player stories, the navigation flow, the card states, and the rules of the Home list; its prototype is built to that file's measurements. The owner's direction is to present it as a shipped feature: no work-in-progress markers, and still no results or metrics, because none have been supplied. The live event is named "Royale" in place of the brand name used in the file. Every other project, cover image and description in the site is a labelled placeholder. Never add testimonials, client logos, awards or numbers until the owner supplies them.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads; everything else is supporting.
2. The whole body of work is always one step away and scannable.
3. Never lose the visitor's place.
4. Scale is a feature: the 30th project is as findable as the first.
5. Honest content: no invented clients, quotes or results.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast ≥4.5:1 (the reference's grey secondary text fails this), full keyboard operation of filters, lightbox and quick-jump, visible focus, motion honouring reduced-motion, project links named by their title.
